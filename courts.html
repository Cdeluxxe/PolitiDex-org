Judicial retention

Separate from the ballot builder. Retention is a yes-or-no question on one judge's name — no opponent, no party, and nothing for PolitiDex to rate.

On your ballot

Only the questions PolitiDex can defend for the location saved in this browser. Statewide retention resolves from your state; a trial-court question resolves from your county. Where the map or roster for a court is not on file, this page names the missing map and names nobody as yours.
