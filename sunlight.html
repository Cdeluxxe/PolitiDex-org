Sunlight Governance

Sunlight Governance tracks how regulated monopolies — such as electric, gas and water utilities — are overseen, by putting rate cases, regulator decisions and related lobbying records into one public, sourced place. The working proof-of-concept is the Philippines Digital Bayanihan Chain, which shows how public records of this kind can be published openly so anyone can follow them.

What can carry this tag

  • Issues about how a regulated utility is overseen
  • Bills that change utility regulation or a commission's process
  • Rate cases before a state public service or utilities commission
  • Lobbying records tied to a regulated utility

On file now
