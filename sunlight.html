☀️ Sunlight Governance

Sunlight Governance

Some systems you can’t opt out of: the power company, the water utility, the roads and the programs your taxes pay for. Sunlight Governance means a full public ledger for them — every cost, every profit and every contract in one open place — so everyone can see the whole picture, head to toe.

What a full ledger shows

  • 🧾CostsWhat it takes to run the system
  • 📈ProfitsWhat the owner is allowed to earn
  • 📜ContractsWho is paid, and on what terms
  • ⚖️DecisionsWhat was asked for, and what was approved

The same idea, later

The same open ledger can later support community programs, where who takes part and what is paid back stay visible to everyone.

  • 🏗️Co-op construction loansEvery loan out, every payment back, on one page.
  • 🌱Community gardensPlots, members and costs in plain view.
  • 🔁Programs that pay themselves backWhat a public program spent, and what it returned.
