Sunlight Banking

Sunlight Banking puts the public record of banks that hold public money or carry a public guarantee in one open place, so anyone can see who holds deposits where and what public backing stands behind them.

What the public record already says

  • The FDIC insures deposits up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category — a public guarantee behind every insured bank.
  • The Utah Department of Financial Institutions charters and supervises Utah state-chartered banks, credit unions and industrial banks.
  • Utah charters more industrial banks (industrial loan companies) than any other state.
  • Public money held by Utah’s state and local treasurers is governed by the State Money Management Act and overseen by the Money Management Council.

Minimum viable ledger

The smallest set of lines that makes this ledger useful. Each line is filled from a public source below.

  • Deposit share by county: which banks hold most of the deposits in each Utah county.
  • Public backing: FDIC insurance, charter type and charter authority for each bank.
  • Public deposits: which banks are eligible to hold Utah public funds.
  • Income and fees: the headline lines of each bank’s quarterly Call Report.
  • Community record: each bank’s latest Community Reinvestment Act rating and its home-lending data.

Market-Power Test: Banking

Three questions to ask of any company or sector. Each one is answered from public records, not opinion.

  1. Choice. Can a customer or taxpayer walk away and use a real alternative?
  2. Concentration. Do a few firms hold most of the market in this place?
  3. Public backing. Does public money, a public charter or a public guarantee stand behind it?

The more of these the record answers “no choice”, “a few firms” or “yes, public backing”, the more a full public ledger helps people see the whole picture.

Utah starting moves

  1. Pull the FDIC Summary of Deposits for Utah and list the top deposit holders in each county.
  2. Match those banks against the Money Management Council’s public list of depositories eligible to hold Utah public funds.
  3. For the largest public-funds depository, file one ledger page: charter, insurance, deposit share, latest Call Report lines and CRA rating, each linked to its source.

Public data sources

Public records and public datasets only. Nothing here asks for or holds private account data.

  • FDIC Summary of Deposits · branch-level deposits, every June
  • FDIC BankFind Suite · charter, insurance and history for every insured bank
  • FFIEC Central Data Repository (Call Reports) · quarterly income, fees, loans and deposits
  • FFIEC Community Reinvestment Act ratings · CRA performance ratings and evaluations
  • HMDA Data Browser · public home-lending data
  • Utah Department of Financial Institutions · Utah charters and supervision
  • Utah State Treasurer (Money Management Council) · rules and depositories for Utah public funds
