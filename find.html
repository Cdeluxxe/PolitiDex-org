Your voting location

Find your districts

Search your address and we name the exact seats you vote in — your U.S. House district, your State Senate district and your State House district — from the district lines themselves, not from your county. That precision is the point: cities like Layton are split across several legislative seats, so a city name alone cannot tell you which one is yours.

PolitiDex resolves your U.S. Senate and Governor seats from your state, anywhere in the country. Your U.S. House, State Senate and State House seats need district lines, which we map in Utah so far — the boundaries on this map come from the Utah Geospatial Resource Center. Outside Utah, set your state and city with "use city / county instead" and we will name every seat we can rather than guess at the ones we cannot. Once your location is saved you can read the seats it names on Who Represents Me, work them on Your Ballot, or open their rooms on District Voice.
